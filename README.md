# PayLine

PayLine is a mobile app that brings every payment a person owes into one place: recurring bills, installment plans, and one-time bills. It shows what is due, sends reminders before each due date, and tracks how much is left to pay. It records and reminds only; it does not process payments.

Course: Information and Communication Architecture

## Phase 1 summary

The full Phase 1 document (Word) is in this repository. This page summarizes its three parts.

 1. App idea
- Name: PayLine
- Concept: One app for all payments, with a dashboard of what is due, reminders, payment history, a calendar, and a monthly summary.
- Purpose: Help people pay on time, avoid late fees, and always know how much is left to pay.
- Target users: Students and young adults, and families tracking household bills.
- Real problem: Payment details are scattered across apps, stores, messages, and memory, so people forget due dates, lose track of installment balances, and pay late.
